Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · LEVoiceCall v1.0.0

LEVoiceCall (“we”, “our”, or “the service”) is a browser-based voice and video calling website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

Account (Facebook Login)

When you sign in with Facebook, we receive only the information you authorize through Facebook Login, typically:

  • Your Facebook user ID
  • Your public display name
  • Your public profile picture URL

We do not request access to your posts, friends list, email (unless Facebook provides it by default and you approve), or other private data beyond public profile.

Call and usage data

  • Call room identifiers (call ID, join code)
  • Call type (voice or video), room name, creator, and participant list for that room
  • Room privacy settings you choose (e.g. screenshot / screen-recording protection flags)
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service (e.g. connection state for WebRTC)

Device permissions

Camera and microphone are accessed only when you create or join a call that needs them, using your browser’s permission prompts. We do not activate camera, microphone, or notifications without your action.

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and show your name and profile picture in the app
  • To create and manage independent call rooms and join codes
  • To connect participants in the same room via WebRTC
  • To enforce creator ownership and room shutdown rules
  • To improve reliability and fix errors

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing of information

  • Other participants in a call can see your display name and profile picture for that room, and hear/see media you share according to the call type and your device controls.
  • Facebook / Meta processes login according to their policies when you use Continue with Facebook.
  • Hosting (e.g. Vercel) may process technical logs as part of running the website.

We do not share your data with advertisers for their own marketing.

4. Data retention

Account session data is stored in your browser (e.g. local storage) until you log out or clear site data. Call room records are kept while a room is active and may be removed when the creator leaves or the room expires. You can request deletion of data associated with your use of the service by contacting us (see below).

5. User data deletion

To request deletion of your LEVoiceCall-related data:

  • Log out of LEVoiceCall and clear this site’s data in your browser settings, and/or
  • Remove the LEVoiceCall app from your Facebook settings (Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites), and/or
  • Email us at the contact address below with the subject “Data deletion request” and your Facebook display name or user ID if known.

We will process reasonable deletion requests within a practical timeframe.

6. Security

We use HTTPS in production. Camera and microphone require a secure context. Call media is exchanged peer-to-peer where possible via WebRTC. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; use the service accordingly.

7. Children’s privacy

LEVoiceCall is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International users

If you access the service from outside the country where our infrastructure is hosted, your information may be processed in other regions. By using LEVoiceCall you acknowledge this transfer as needed to provide the service.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
